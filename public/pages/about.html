About LCPGameStats

A personal project to track and explore your game library.

Back to library

LCPGameStats is a web application that connects to Steam and other providers to visualize your game library, playtime, and achievements in one place.

Features

  • Multi-provider support: Steam, Epic Games, GOG, Uplay, and RetroAchievements
  • Multiple themes including Dark, Light, Glassmorphism, Liquid Glass, and Neomorphism
  • Detailed achievement tracking with filters and search
  • Export your library data to JSON or XML
  • Secure accounts with local login or Google sign-in
